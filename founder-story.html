My Story

The story behind MyKurda, and why I decided to build it.

My name is Mohamad Saleh Abdulrahman, and I’m a student software developer building MyKurda.

I wrote my first lines of code in August 2023. At the time, I was simply learning how to make things work. A few years later, I found myself wanting to build something of my own — something that could be useful to people beyond just the screen in front of me.

That idea became MyKurda.

I came to the Netherlands in 2015 as a Kurdish refugee, leaving behind a difficult chapter of my life. Growing up between different places made me appreciate something I had always carried with me: language is more than words.

It is the stories we inherit.

The way our parents speak to us.

The jokes that only make sense in your own language.

The words that somehow feel different when you hear them from home.

I wanted to build something that could help keep that connection alive.

MyKurda is my answer to that idea.

I don't want Kurdish to feel like something people only study from a textbook. I want it to feel alive — something you can learn through games, discover through stories, share with other people, and actually enjoy using.

And I want to build it for everyone: for people who already speak Kurdish, for people trying to learn it, and especially for those who grew up hearing it but never had the chance to truly learn it.

I'm building MyKurda while I'm still a student. I'm learning, making mistakes, rebuilding things, and figuring out a little more every day.

There is no big company behind this yet.

It's just an idea, a lot of code, and someone who believes it is worth building.

The app isn't here yet.

But I'm working on it.

And if everything goes the way I hope, MyKurda will become more than an app for learning Kurdish.

It will become a place where the language can grow, be shared, and stay alive for the next generation.

MyKurda is coming soon.This is only the beginning.
